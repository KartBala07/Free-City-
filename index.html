San Francisco, in 3D.

Real street, coastline, and building footprints from OpenStreetMap via OpenFreeMap. Building heights use mapped values when available; other heights are estimates. Hills use terrain elevation at 1× scale.

Golden Gate Bridge, Coit Tower, and the Ferry Building have simplified landmark models. This is a geographic reconstruction, not a photographic or survey-grade replica. Painted Ladies and other neighborhoods use mapped building volumes, without individual façade textures.

The 50 simulated citizens occupy a downtown district. Their movement is illustrative and does not yet follow San Francisco’s street network. Simulation weather and time are fictional.

Map tiles and elevation need an internet connection. Classic view keeps the original offline-capable schematic renderer available.
